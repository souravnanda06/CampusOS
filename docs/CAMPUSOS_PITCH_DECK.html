Core Cryptography
🔑

Device-Bound Keypair

Non-Exportable
  • During onboarding, the client generates a 32-byte Ed25519 cryptographic keypair directly on the smartphone.
  • The private key is stored in hardware-backed secure storage and never leaves the device.
  • Single-device binding policy: enrolling a new phone immediately invalidates the previous key on the server.
32-Byte Ed25519 Curve25519 Asymmetric Key
🔄

30s Rotating Dynamic QR

Anti-Screenshot
  • The student app generates a fresh QR presentation every 30 seconds.
  • Payload: Signature(UUID + Nonce + Epoch_Timestamp).
  • Screenshots taken and forwarded to friends expire in <30 seconds and fail verification.
  • Biometric gate (FaceID / Fingerprint) required to unlock the passport.
30 Sec Dynamic QR token expiration window
⚡

Offline Trust Bundles

0 Bytes Internet
  • Teacher and verifier devices cache institutional public keys in local SQLite Trust Bundles.
  • Signature validation executes completely offline in under 2 milliseconds without contacting the server.
  • Consumed nonces are stored locally and synced upstream to permanently block replay attempts (HTTP 409).
< 2 ms Local offline signature verification time

P2P Protocols

  • Emergency Circulars: Designed for urgent text notices, timetable amendments, and disaster evacuation maps when campus Wi-Fi is dead.
  • 100% Radio Autonomous: Functions in the middle of a forest, during a flood, or in a deep basement with no routers.
≤ 2 MB Pure BLE multi-hop document transfer limit

Tier 2: Local Wi-Fi AirDrop (≤ 100 MB at 50 MB/s)

LocalSend Engine
  • LocalSend Protocol Integration: Broadcasts peer discovery packets over open UDP Port 53317 across the local campus router.
  • High-Speed TCP Socket Streaming: Streams large textbooks, software archives, and lecture videos at up to 50 MB/second.
  • Zero Cloud Egress: Packets flow entirely within the local Wi-Fi switch—never touching external telecom fiber or consuming university ISP data.
  • Saves Lakhs in Cloud Bills: Eliminates AWS S3 bandwidth fees for distributing syllabus materials across 5,000 students.
